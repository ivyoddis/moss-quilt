What is a collective moss quilt?
A Collective Moss Quilt is a living archive of slow walks and small discoveries. Every piece of this quilt was uploaded by someone who stopped, looked down, and touched the moss. Together these moments become something larger — proof that softness and slowness still exist. Moss facts: + Moss has no roots. It attaches itself gently to whatever surface it finds and holds on slowly, over time. + Moss doesn't compete. It simply finds a forgotten corner and makes something beautiful there. + A single square inch of moss can contain hundreds of microscopic creatures living entire lives you'll never see. + Moss is a sign of clean air. Where moss thrives, the world is a little healthier.
